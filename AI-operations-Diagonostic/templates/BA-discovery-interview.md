# Customer discovery interview (35 minutes)

Use only with permission. Do not request raw personal, confidential or regulated data over email. Record anonymised role, date, permission, evidence source and follow-up owner.

- **0–5 min:** Which recurring operational process has been hardest to scale? Why now? Who owns it?
- **5–12 min:** Walk through the last real case, trigger, roles, handoffs and completion criteria.
- **12–20 min:** Monthly volume, active handling time vs elapsed time, queues, exceptions, rework and peak variation. How were these measured?
- **20–26 min:** Errors, SLA breaches, costs, compliance exposure and prior improvement attempts. What changed and how was impact measured?
- **26–31 min:** Current software, security restrictions, approved evidence scope and decision-makers.
- **31–35 min:** Would an evidence-based diagnostic change a decision? Who signs off, what price/ROI evidence is required, and what would prevent purchase?

## Required follow-ups

Ask for a last-case example, minimum/maximum cycle time, sample selection bias, active vs elapsed time, labour-rate basis, exception path, and whether released capacity could actually become cash savings.

## Record

- Workflow and start/end boundaries
- Volumes, sample size, period and source
- Active minutes vs queue time
- Exception/rework rate and denominator
- Fully loaded labour cost basis
- Systems, files, sensitivity and consent
- Buyer authority, budget evidence, objections and next action
